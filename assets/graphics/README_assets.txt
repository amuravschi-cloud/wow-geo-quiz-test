Flags
- Source: flag-icons v7.5.0
- URL: https://github.com/lipis/flag-icons/tree/v7.5.0
- License: MIT; license text included as LICENSE_flag-icons_MIT.txt.
- SVG originals are in flags/svg. PNG 512px derivatives are generated locally.
- National symbols may be subject to country-specific rules on official or misleading use.

Outline maps
- Source: Natural Earth 1:10m Admin 0 Countries, version 5.1.1.
- URL: https://www.naturalearthdata.com/downloads/10m-cultural-vectors/10m-admin-0-countries/
- License: Public Domain.
- SVG outlines are generated from the source GeoJSON; PNG 512px derivatives are generated locally.
- Natural Earth uses a de facto boundary policy. Disputed boundaries must not be used for politically framed questions without a separate review.

Coverage
- Scope: 193 UN members plus the Holy See and the State of Palestine.
- The asset manifest records every file, source and license.
